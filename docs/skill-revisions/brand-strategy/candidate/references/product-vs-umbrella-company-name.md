# Product name vs umbrella company name

Use this when a session moves from naming one product to naming the company, studio, open-source organization, or long-lived portfolio that will own it.

## Decision boundary

- A product name should fit its capability, CLI, package, repository, daemon, provider key, and container image.
- An umbrella name should remain credible across multiple products, games, research work, and future business lines for roughly ten years.
- Do not promote a successful product name into the company name automatically.
- Do not force a founder surname plus a generic suffix such as `Labs` after the user rejects that naming pattern; preserve it in the eliminated-direction list.

## Workflow

1. Record the product name as a settled child-brand decision.
2. Open a separate umbrella naming track with its own positioning question and acceptance owner.
3. Generate candidates internally, then apply the lightweight software/brand collision preflight before showing them.
4. If domain feasibility is a delivery requirement, verify the approved domain pattern before presenting candidates. Otherwise reserve deep domain, social, GitHub-organization, and trademark research for human-selected finalists.
5. Make organization creation structurally dependent on the umbrella-name decision; do not create permanent infrastructure under a disposable placeholder.
6. Treat domain registration, trademark filing, and company formation as separate approval/spending tasks.

## Access-planning note

When the chosen umbrella will own public repositories, keep the organization owner role human-controlled. Existing automation/user identities may receive member access according to the user's stated model, but never create a second identity or broaden owner/security scope silently when the exact account type cannot support the requested role.
